# Istana central-portico reference brief

Status: **public architectural reference only; not survey/as-built evidence and
not permission to reproduce protected symbols or restricted details**.

The current V8 overlay is a bounded front-facing interpretation. Official and
NHB text supports only the broad architectural vocabulary, symmetry and
projected-portico reading described below. Exact repeat counts, member ordering
and fitted proportions come only from the two admitted, hash-bound 2009 CC BY
2.0 fitting images. Project-generated captures are diagnosis and QA evidence,
not geometry truth.

## Authoritative public features

The Istana's official building page describes the Main Building as a tropical
Neo-Palladian composition with wide verandahs, louvred windows and panelled
doors. It records a central three-storey tower with a slated mansard roof,
dormer windows, Corinthian pilasters and cornices; the wings use Ionic
colonnades above Doric colonnades, with pedimented porticoes at the ends:
<https://www.istana.gov.sg/visit-and-explore/buildings/>.

The National Heritage Board description adds that the symmetrical front is
dominated by a projected central portico, flanked by twin porticoes with
triangular pediments, and identifies arches, architraves, cornices, louvres,
verandahs and classical orders as defining elements:
<https://www.roots.gov.sg/places/places-landing/Places/national-monuments/the-istana-and-sri-temasek>.

These descriptions support the general vocabulary and composition checks below.
They do not establish exact repeat counts, dimensions, depth, material
calibration or the April 2024 state.

## Frozen V8 fitting evidence

`portico_depth_evidence_v8.json` binds the two admitted 2009 CC BY 2.0 fitting
images by path, source page, bytes and SHA-256. Those images support the V8
interpretation of three lower reveal assemblies, three upper screens, four
paired upper-column groups, three sill balustrades and their vertical layer
ordering. These are bounded fitting observations, not official, surveyed or
as-built facts.

The three Explore V3 captures diagnose the wide, empty centre and are used only
to judge the rendered response. They supply no dimensions or hidden geometry.
The frozen evidence records `officialSitePixelsRead=false`: only the textual
descriptions above are used from the official and NHB pages. Reading new image
pixels from those sites requires prior rights review, immutable source hashes,
held-out-review rules and a newly approved source contract.

## V8 capture checks

At axial and left/right oblique views, require:

- the projected central portico reads as a grounded three-dimensional volume,
  not an applied flat façade;
- the overlay's three lower reveal assemblies remain visually uncapped and have
  coherent reveals, soffits and rear depth cues;
- paired columns/pilasters have consistent bases, shafts, capitals and contact;
- the upper louvred bays read as ventilated tropical openings rather than flat
  painted rectangles;
- cornice, balustrade, upper bays and lower arcade align symmetrically on the
  central axis;
- no double geometry, z-fighting, floating trim, unsupported overhang, stair
  seam or inherited V7 leakage;
- the overlay does not imply surveyed side/rear depth, exact marble, restoration
  state or structural construction.

This visual test says nothing about the real aperture state. The openings may be
open, glazed, shuttered or door-backed. The inherited collision/render backing
is unvalidated and must not be removed or labelled false merely because the V8
overlay leaves its own reveals uncapped.

The flanking pedimented porticoes, full wing orders, mansard tower and dormers
belong to the inherited full-building composition. Do not force them into the
central overlay merely because the public descriptions mention them.

## Symbol and source restrictions

The official Istana page states that use of Presidential symbols, including the
Presidential Crest and Standard, must be approved by the President. External
organisations are directed to write to the President's Office to seek that
approval:
<https://www.istana.gov.sg/The-President/Presidents-Portrait-and-Insignia/Portrait/>.
The overlay must therefore retain no crest/standard unless written permission
and the approved artwork/use conditions are recorded.

Under the frozen V8 contract, official and NHB image pixels are not inspected at
all. They must not be traced, projected, baked into textures or redistributed as
project assets. Metric refinement still requires separately authorised,
hash-bound oblique/side capture, control points or record drawings through
`ISTANA_DATA_REQUEST_DRAFTS.md`.

The Istana site's current Terms of Use state that its contents, graphics and
images are protected and may not be reproduced, modified, appropriated or
distributed without prior written permission. They also require permission
before publicly hyperlinking to or framing the site. The page addresses above
are internal evidence locators only; do not publish this brief or those links
without the required permission:
<https://www.istana.gov.sg/terms-of-use/>.

The Roots/NHB Terms of Use separately prohibit reproducing, copying, publishing,
adapting or distributing Roots content without NHB's prior written permission.
The NHB URL above is likewise an internal textual evidence locator, not a grant
to reuse its photographs or other content:
<https://www.roots.gov.sg/terms-of-use>.

## Acceptance boundary

A capture can prove that the overlay is compositionally richer and more
consistent with the published Neo-Palladian/tropical vocabulary. It cannot
prove exact likeness, metric depth, material truth, restoration epoch,
structural solidity, collision correctness or RF aperture state. Keep those
claims false until the corresponding owner/survey evidence exists.

Acceptance is limited to the frozen front and bounded left/right-oblique QA
views; it is not complete-orbit acceptance. A passing capture does not authorize
installation of the frozen V8 source package, replacement or mutation of V7,
concurrent V7/V8 rendering, public distribution or production promotion. Keep
`completeOrbitAccepted`, `liveInstallAuthorized`,
`productionPromotionAuthorized`, `publicDistributionAuthorized`,
`replaceOrMutateFrozenV5OrV7` and `v7AndV8ConcurrentRenderingAuthorized` false
unless a separate recorded authority changes the corresponding contract.
